World, region & stage maps (v1.0)

Drawn by Opus. Tall scroll paintings (two screens tall, scroll vertically). The game draws the UI on top: path beads, zone nodes, boss medallion, ground pins, seals, locks and fog (all from the UI kit). Regions are shown with their floating islands placed in the fixed slots; stage maps are shown with the UI nodes on the fixed anchors (gate → Zone 1 → 2 → 3 → boss lair, Cultivation Ground on the side branch).
