# 00 — Content Registry (single source of truth)

Every other content file uses **only** the names, tiers, and unlock points defined here. To add something new, add it here first.

Realm abbreviations (**Mortal Plane**, the launch ladder): **MO** Meridian Opening · **QC** Qi Condensation · **QS** Qi Sea · **FE** Foundation Establishment · **GC** Golden Core · **NS** Nascent Soul · **SF** Soul Formation · **VB** Void Breaking · **MA** Mahayana · **GA** Great Ascension.
Later Planes (Immortal, God, Chaos, ...) are in [16-Planes-Ascension](16-Planes-Ascension.md).

**Tier T1–T9** = the content band where something first matters:

| Tier | T1 | T2 | T3 | T4 | T5 | T6 | T7 | T8 | T9 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Realms | MO, QC, QS | FE | GC | NS | SF | VB | MA | GA 1–4 | GA 5–9 |

*Renamed from the old ladder: Qi Refining → split into MO/QC/QS · Core Formation → Golden Core · Void Refining → Void Breaking · Body Integration → removed (its T7 content moves to Mahayana) · old Mahayana content → GA early (T8) · Tribulation Transcendence → GA late (T9).*
