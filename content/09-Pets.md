# 09 — Pets (Spirit Beasts)

**Unlock:** NS 1 (see [00-Registry](00-Registry.md)). **Milestone:** M2 (T1–T6 pets), M3 (T7–T9 pets).
Pets are kept through Rebirth.

## Slots
| Slot | Unlock | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Active pet | NS 1 | Fights alongside you (its assist), **and** gives its passive bonus |
| Passive slot 1 | NS 1 | Passive bonus only |
| Passive slot 2 | SF 1 | Passive bonus only |
| Passive slot 3 | VB 1 | Passive bonus only |

- The active pet can be targeted by enemies (it has HP = 30% of yours). If it's knocked out, it returns next fight. Taunt arts protect it.
- **Assist power** = your ATK × (0.2 + 0.005 × pet level) × stage multiplier. It fires on its own cooldown.
- **Stage multipliers** (assist and passive): Stage 1 ×1.0 · Stage 2 ×1.5 · Stage 3 ×2.25.
- **Shared pools ([02-Systems §0](../02-Systems.md)):** the pet's own HP is separate and resets each fight. **Your** HP and Qi are the shared Vessel pools, so:
  - **Healer pets are allowed and intended.** In battle, HP only has slow **base regen** (10% of the Train rate; [02-Systems §0b](../02-Systems.md)), so healer assists (Jade Rabbit, Star Whale Calf, Taotie Pup's lifesteal) are the **main in-battle healing, alongside Healing Pills**. They restore your **real HP pool**, push back the retreat point, and lengthen every Battle step. That makes them the go-to active pets for loot-focused players. Heals are always a **% of your Max HP**, so they scale with the body.
  - **Taunt/shield pets** (Iron-Back Turtle line) reduce the damage you take, which also lengthens Battle steps.
  - Assists only fire in combat. Pets don't heal you during Train or Meditate (training already refills the pools).
  - Passive **+Qi %** bonuses raise the Qi rate (refill and growth) and stack multiplicatively with the Talisman. **+Max Qi %** (Kraken Spawn) raises the pool size and the cap headroom for combat arts.

## Pet Leveling
- **Level caps:** Stage 1 → 30, Stage 2 → 60, Stage 3 → 90.
- **XP sources:**
  - **Pet food**, cooked with the Cooking skill (recipes in [08-Life-Skills](08-Life-Skills.md)): Beast Chow (T1), Spirit Beast Feast (T3), Star-Grade Beast Feast (T5), Dragon-Grade Beast Feast (T6+). Food of a higher tier than the pet gives +50% XP.
  - **Beast Cores** can be fed directly at 25% of the XP of the same-tier Pet Food.
  - Duplicate pets: see Bond below.
- XP to next level: `20 × 1.12^level` (tune in the balancing spreadsheet).
- Passive bonuses grow +1% (relative) per pet level within a stage.

## Bond (duplicates)
Getting a pet you already own adds +1 **Bond** (max 5). Each Bond level gives +10% to that pet's passive. Duplicates past Bond 5 turn into Beast Cores (×10 of the pet's tier).

## Obtaining Pets

### Eggs (D3 Beast Valley)
- D3 drops **Spirit Beast Eggs (T1–T9)**. The tier matches the dungeon difficulty tier you cleared.
- An egg hatches into a random pet from that tier's **egg pool** (Spirit Fox, Jade Rabbit, Iron-Back Turtle, Thunder Sparrow, Void Moth; plus Azure Dragon Hatchling from D6 eggs only). Higher rarity is rarer.
- **Hatching** takes real time (1 h × egg tier) in the **Incubator** (1 slot, +1 from the Karma perk tree). Jade can speed it up.
- Bosses also have a small chance to drop an egg of their tier (registry: Spirit Beast Egg).

### Capture (map bosses)
- Bosses with a capture pet (see the roster) roll a **capture chance after each kill**, including rematches.

| Pet rarity | Base capture chance | Chance doubles after | Guaranteed by |
|---|---|---|---|
| Spirit | 1% | 50 kills | 150 kills |
| Earth | 0.5% | 100 kills | 300 kills |
| Heaven | 0.2% | 200 kills | 600 kills |
| Immortal | 0.05% | 400 kills | 1,200 kills |

- The pity counter is per boss, shown in that boss's info panel, and kept through Rebirth.
- Capture needs an **empty pet roster** spot (roster size: 20, so capture is never blocked unless the player owns every pet).
- Captures start at Stage 1, level 1.

## Pet UI
Inside the Character tab, a **Pets** sub-tab with: slot board (1 active + 3 passive), roster grid (silhouettes for undiscovered pets, with a source hint), a feed button (auto-select best food), evolve, Incubator, and pity counters.

## Decisions
- **No pet equipment.**
- **Element advantage:** the active pet uses the Wu Xing cycle like the player. **+10% damage** against an element it counters.
- **Pet expeditions** (send passive pets to gather): post-launch.
- **Healer pets are allowed** and intended as the main in-battle sustain. Heals are a % of Max HP (Jade Rabbit 5% / 8 s at Stage 1, ×1.5 and ×2.25 at later stages). Tune only if Battle steps never reach the retreat point.
