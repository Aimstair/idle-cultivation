## Shared Rules (all 8 dungeons)

### Entries
| Source | Entries | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Free daily | **3 per dungeon** | Reset at 04:00 local server day |
| Rewarded ad | +1 per dungeon | Max **2 ad entries/day across all dungeons** (05-Monetization cap) |
| Jade | +1 per purchase (20 → 40 Jade) | Max **2/day across all dungeons** |
| Weekend bonus (LiveOps) | +1 per dungeon | Sat–Sun |

Unused entries **don't** stack.

### Run structure
- **10 waves + boss.** Waves 1–9 use 3–6 monsters drawn from the dungeon's pool. Wave 5 is an **elite** (one monster ×3 HP). Wave 10 is a **mini-boss**.
- **Enter with your current pools.** A run starts with whatever HP and Qi you have now (shared Vessel pools, [02-Systems §0](../02-Systems.md)). HP, Qi, statuses, and pill cooldowns carry over between waves, and **your pools stay depleted after the run.** The entry screen warns below 70% HP or Qi.
- **No retreat point inside a run.** Dungeons count as the Battle activity, so only slow base regen applies (10% of the Meditate/Train rate). Reaching 0 HP ends the run (a loss) and adds one **Internal Injury** stack (5 min). Healer pets and Healing Pills are the only in-run heals.
- **Consumable slot:** 1 Healing Pill stack and 1 buff pill. Auto-used at <30% HP or at run start.
- **Time limit:** 3 min total. The boss enrages at 60 s into its fight (A7).
- **Loss:** the run ends, cleared waves pay **50%**, and the entry is consumed.

### Difficulty tiers
Each dungeon has **5 tiers (I–V)**. Tier I unlocks with the dungeon. Each further tier unlocks **2 levels** into the realm (or later), and is gated by clearing the previous tier.

| Tier | Monster power (vs Tier I) | Reward multiplier | Recommended |
|---|---|---|---|
| I | ×1 | ×1 | Realm Lvl 1–2 |
| II | ×2.2 | ×1.6 | Lvl 3–4 |
| III | ×4.8 | ×2.5 | Lvl 5–6 |
| IV | ×10 | ×4 | Lvl 7–8 |
| V | ×22 | ×6, + exclusive drop table | Lvl 9 / next realm |

### Stars & Sweep
| Star | Condition |
|---|---|
| ★ | Clear |
| ★★ | Clear with ≥50% HP remaining |
| ★★★ | Clear in under 90 s |

- **Sweep:** after clearing a tier once (**★**), *Sweep* spends an entry and grants **100% of normal rewards** instantly (no first-clear bonus). This is the idle-friendly path.
- **Sweep All:** unlocked at SF (or Karma perk). It spends all remaining entries on your highest cleared tiers at once. Higher ★ ratings give first-time bonus rewards and achievements, not Sweep access.
- **First-clear bonus** per tier: Jade (20 / 30 / 50 / 80 / 120) plus a guaranteed rare item from the exclusive table.

### Weekly modifiers (rotate Monday, all dungeons)
| Modifier | Effect | Bonus |
|---|---|---|
| Elemental Surge (one element) | Monsters of that element +30% ATK | +20% materials |
| Iron Wall | All monsters +50% DEF | +20% stones |
| Swarm Tide | +2 monsters per wave | +25% Beast Cores |
| Frenzy | Monsters +30% SPD | +1 extra boss loot roll |
| Barren Qi | Your Qi regen halved during the run | +20% Manual Fragments / eggs |
| Calm (no modifier) | — | — |

Modifiers are shown on the entry screen. The rewards bonus applies to sweeps too.

## Trial Tower (endless) · GC 1 · M2
**Purpose:** an endless power benchmark, a weekly goal, and the future leaderboard (M3).

### Rules
- **No entry limit.** Climb floor by floor until you lose. Each floor is a **single fight** (1–3 monsters). Every **10th floor is a boss** (a reused map boss with tower scaling).
- Monster power: `floor 1 ≈ GC 1 map zone 1`, then **×1.08 per floor**. The element and archetype mix rotates by floor.
- **Enter with your current pools.** No full heal between floors (only base regen). HP and Qi carry over, and stay depleted when you stop. Every 10th floor restores 30% HP and Qi. A loss (0 HP) causes **Internal Injury**. Tip: climb after a Meditate + Train session, with full pools.
- **Not simulated offline.** It's an active-play feature. A **"Climb"** button auto-fights until the first loss (seconds to resolve, with a skippable animation).
- **Weekly reset:** every Monday the tower resets to your **checkpoint** (the last multiple of 10 below your best floor, minus 20).

### Rewards
| Type | Reward |
|---|---|
| First clear of each floor | Spirit Stones (scales with floor) |
| First clear of each 10th floor | 30 Jade + Refining Stones ×5 |
| Every 50th floor (first time) | Title + cosmetic aura piece, Dao Pattern Stone ×3 |
| **Weekly chest** (by highest floor this week) | Tiered: floors 10/25/50/75/100/150+ give Jade (20 → 200), Manual Fragments, Spirit Beast Egg (100+) |

### Leaderboard (M3)
- Weekly ranking by highest floor, then fastest clear time as the tiebreaker. Brackets of 100 players by realm.
- Top 10% get a cosmetic frame for the week. Rewards are cosmetic only (no power), in line with 05-Monetization.

## Decisions
- Entries are **per dungeon** (3/day each), not a shared pool.
- Sweep needs only **★** (one clear).
- Void Labyrinth paths auto-pick by default, with a manual setting.
- Trial Tower stays active play (no offline auto-climb). It's the competitive leaderboard.
- Rebirth **resets** dungeon tier progress. The *Dungeon Sweeper* Karma perk keeps past tiers sweepable.
