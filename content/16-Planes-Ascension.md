# 16 — Planes & Ascension

The full cultivation ladder has **65 realms in 10 Planes**. Each Plane is a self-contained chapter with its own realms, maps, materials tier, and signature system. Finishing a Plane triggers **Ascension**, a one-way move to the next Plane, and the second prestige layer above Rebirth.

## How a Plane works
- **Realms:** same rules as the Mortal Plane (Vessel Model, 9 levels, gate pills, tribulations).
- **Maps & grounds:** about 2–3 combat maps per realm, each unlocking a Meditation Site or Training Ground ([15-Cultivation-Grounds](15-Cultivation-Grounds.md)).
- **Materials tier:** each Plane has its own material and gear grade (Mortal T1–T9, Immortal I1–I9, and so on).
- **Body track:** each Plane adds new body stages. Immortal Plane example: Immortal Bone → Immortal Marrow → Immortal Blood → Immortal Body Perfected.
- **Rebirth inside a Plane:** a rebirth sends you back to the **first realm of your current Plane**, never back to the Mortal Plane. Rebirth gains and stacking Karma use that Plane's realms.

## Ascension (the second prestige layer)
### Requirements
- **Peak of the Plane's last realm** (for example, Great Ascension 9).
- **Ascension Pill** (Alchemy 95), or the Plane's own ascension item.
- **Body:** at least the Plane's second-to-last body stage (Mortal Plane: Diamond Vajra Body).
- Clear the **final tribulation** (Mortal Plane: Heavenly Dao Judgement).

### What happens
| Reset (left behind in the old Plane) | Kept |
|---|---|
| Realm, level, Qi and HP pools | Karma, perk tree, and Karma still earned in new Planes |
| Body stage (you start the new Plane's first body stage) | Exercise mastery levels |
| **Rebirth Qi and Body multipliers** (back to ×1; converted to Dao Marks, below) | Life-skill levels, recipes, workers |
| Map, ground, and dungeon progress for the old Plane | Pets, techniques, achievements, titles, Jade, cosmetics, IAP perks |
| Spirit Stones (converted to the new Plane's currency at a fixed rate) | Gear and swords (usable, but outclassed by the new grade; can be refined into upgrade materials) |

### What you gain
- **Dao Marks** (permanent, never reset):
  ```
  Dao Marks = floor( 10 × √(Qi multiplier × Body multiplier) )     at the moment of Ascension
  Each Dao Mark: +1% Qi rate, Tempering, and damage (additive), forever
  ```
  Example: ×30 Qi and ×15 Body give √450 ≈ 21.2, so **212 Dao Marks** (+212%). The square root compresses huge Mortal multipliers so they can't trivialize the next Plane, while still rewarding a strong final life.
- **The next Plane's content:** new realms, maps, grounds, dungeons, and its signature system.
- **An Ascension title and visual** (aura, robe) per Plane.

### Karma and old Planes
- **Karma and perks are never reset** by Ascension.
- **Old Planes stay visitable.** You can travel back to farm their maps and dungeons for old materials, collections, achievements, and pet captures. Old grounds still work, at their original (now weak) multipliers. Old bosses don't scale up, so it's fast farming.

### Why players do it
Rebirth multipliers in a Plane eventually plateau against the last realms' walls. Ascension turns that plateau into permanent Dao Marks and opens a new chapter. Like Rebirth, *when* to ascend is a choice: one more strong rebirth before ascending gives more Dao Marks.

## Pacing targets (typical daily player, first time through)
| Plane | Realms | Time to complete | Cumulative |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mortal | 10 | ~6 months | ~6 months |
| Immortal | 10 | ~6 months | ~1 year |
| Sovereign | 7 | ~4–5 months | ~1.4 years |
| Divine | 8 | ~5 months | ~1.8 years |
| Enlightened | 5 | ~3 months | ~2.1 years |
| Chaos | 5 | ~3 months | ~2.3 years |
| Dao | 5 | ~3 months | ~2.6 years |
| Divinity | 6 | ~3–4 months | ~2.9 years |
| Reality | 6 | ~3–4 months | ~3.2 years |
| Origin | 2 | ~2 months | **~3.3–3.5 years** |

- Planes 5–10 are deliberately **shorter**, keeping the source list's 2–6 realms, with depth coming from each Plane's signature system.
- Dao Marks speed up every later Plane, so dedicated players will be faster, and they'll usually catch up to the newest released Plane.
- **The real limit is the release cadence.** At about one Plane per 6 months, the Origin Plane ships about **4.5 years after launch**. Players who join late can catch up.

## Decisions
- **Planes 5–10:** shorter (the list's 2–6 realms each), with depth from each Plane's signature system.
- **Fire & Wind Calamity:** gates Earth Immortal → Heaven Immortal.
- **Ascension** never resets Karma or perks. **Old Planes** stay visitable.
- Signature systems stay as an outline until the Mortal Plane design is final.
