## What the player actually sees (lesson from the pilot)
In the game the hero (a ~236×354 px painted figure) sits in the middle and covers most of x 77–313, y 200–554. In the pilot the hermitage hut ended up hidden behind the hero and a gauge. So for every ground:
- Keep the area directly behind the hero (x 120–270, y 200–540) **calm and backlit**. It is mostly hidden and only needs to glow.
- Put the place's **identity in big, readable silhouettes that show AROUND the hero**: a wide roofline, arch, cliff, tree canopy, waterfall or structure spanning behind and ABOVE his shoulders (y 150–300, wide across x 60–330), and/or tall elements just inside the rails beside the gauges (x 100–150 and x 240–290).
- Put **foreground identity** (water, stepping stones, railings, rocks, props) at y 545–640 on the left and right thirds (x 0–140 and x 250–390). The centre below y 560 is covered by the location ribbon and the Break Through plaque.
- **Training Grounds:** the hero stands or trains instead of sitting, so the centre seat becomes a **training floor** (flat stone or wooden floor, top surface around y 530–550). Place the ground's **exercise prop** (named per ground) visibly beside the hero, at x 100–150 or 240–300, y 380–560.

## File rules
viewBox `0 0 390 844`, width 780, height 1688, opaque, no text, no characters. Ids prefixed with the ground number (`g02-`, `g03-` …). **Make them as detailed and beautiful as possible** (the user's request): 3–5 sub-depths with atmospheric perspective, real texture (rock strata, bark, roof tiles, plank grain, water ripples and reflections, cloth), a clear key light with rim light and god rays or glows, small story props and statically drawn particles (petals, embers, snow, fireflies, spores). The approved battle maps in `battle/map02_*.svg` … `map17_*.svg` show the detail level wanted. Up to ~200 KB per file is fine (the game converts to PNG); avoid pathological blur. Keep previews cheap: render at 390×844, look, fix, re-render once — no long render loops.
